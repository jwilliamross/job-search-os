# Profile (source of truth for every claim in a letter or resume)

Fill this in once. Every number, date, title, and name Claude uses in an outbound document
must trace to a line here or to something you said in the session. Tag anything you are
not sure of with `[VERIFY]`; Claude will not use it until you confirm.

## Contact
- Name, city, email, phone, LinkedIn URL, work eligibility.

## Education
- School, program, expected graduation, GPA (cumulative and by term), honours, awards,
  relevant courses with grades, certifications.

## Work experience (one block per role)
- Employer, title, dates, supervisor (for references), what you actually did with numbers,
  what you were praised for, what you would rather not claim.

## Projects, ventures, side businesses
- What it is, what you built, what is live, what it earned. Be exact about what is real.

## Skills, honestly rated
- Tools you use daily, tools you can direct AI to use, tools you cannot use yet.

## Stories (see stories.md)

## Network
- People who would take your call, by firm. Put the LinkedIn connections export in
  `profile/network/`.

## Rules you set for Claude
- What never goes in a letter (family, health, finances, an injury).
- What always goes in.
- How you want to be described, in order.
