# Voice guide (fill from your own writing, not from AI-assisted drafts)

Put three to six pieces you wrote entirely yourself in `samples/own-writing/`: essays,
emails, anything. Then answer, with examples from those pieces:

- How do you open? (Frame sentence first, or straight in?)
- Contractions: yes or no in formal writing?
- Typical sentence length, and how much you vary it.
- Punctuation habits: semicolons, colons before a reveal, parentheses, rhetorical questions.
- Words and phrases you actually use. Words you never use.
- How you talk about yourself: numbers, verbs, adjectives? Bragging style or understated?
- Sign-offs you use in email.

Claude reads this and the samples before every draft, and the humanizer skill uses the
samples as its voice reference.
