# The interview

Answer by voice or in writing, messy is fine. Claude turns the answers into PROFILE.md,
VOICE.md, TARGETS.md, and stories.md.

## Next 12 months
1. Rank the role types you want, honestly, and say why.
2. Which terms, which cities, what pay floor, what eligibility constraints.
3. What happened the last time you applied widely: replies, interviews, offers, and your
   own diagnosis of why.

## Each past job or venture
4. Who you reported to, the size of the operation, what you actually did each day.
5. The two or three outputs someone there would vouch for, with numbers.
6. What you would not want claimed on your behalf.
7. Who there would be a reference.

## School
8. GPA by term, honours, awards, best course and why, worst course and why.
9. Clubs, competitions, leadership roles, and which of them you want used.

## Skills, honestly
10. What you can do by hand, what you can do with AI, what you cannot do yet.

## Stories
11. A failure and what changed. Leading people older than you. A disagreement with a boss.
    The hardest thing you have done. Something you built from nothing. A time you learned
    fast under a deadline. A problem nobody had noticed.

## Motivation
12. Why this field, in your own words, without the word "passion."
13. Firms on your list and anyone you know inside them.

## Boundaries
14. What never goes in a letter. How you want to be described, in order.

## Working with Claude
15. How you want drafts delivered, how much you want to edit yourself, whether to disclose
    AI use and show the prompt, and for which employers.
